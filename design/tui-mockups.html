keycron — TUI design directions

Terminal-accurate mockups (box-drawing, Catppuccin Mocha palette, real M6 data). Pick a direction, then I build it in ratatui.

A · Tabbed workspace
Top tab bar, one focused panel at a time, command footer. Familiar (gitui / lazygit). Scales to many sections.

Everything visible at once in tiled panels; click/hotkey a tile to edit inline. Dense, "control panel" feel (btop / bottom).
